# Afterhall

**Your voice, still in the room.**

Afterhall is a self-hosted personal archive that works as your assistant today and becomes a legacy for your family tomorrow.

- **Data before model.** Everything lives in open formats (Markdown, JSON Lines, audio). Models get swapped, the archive stays.
- **Self-hosted first.** Your archive runs on your own server or NAS and keeps working even if this project disappears.
- **Two modes, one archive.** The assistant may think along with you. The legacy mode only answers with a source or a date, and always says that you are talking to a likeness.
- **Third parties are protected.** Only your own messages shape the persona. Other people's messages are pseudonymised and never quoted.
- **Your export is never paywalled.** The archive export and the format specification are always free.

> ⚠️  Afterhall is in early development. Repositories are private until the first public release. Before that release, the security-critical core (key sharing, dead man’s switch, vault separation) will be reviewed by an independent party.
